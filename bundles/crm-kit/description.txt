Short: LimeSurvey and SuiteCRM, linked so survey responses become leads and cases, with an AI Assistant in your browser.
Extended: The Customer Kit is LimeSurvey 7 and SuiteCRM, already linked. You build a survey in LimeSurvey, choose which question fills which CRM field, and switch the link on for that survey. Each completed response then arrives in SuiteCRM as a lead or a case. A demo survey shows how. The AI Assistant works on both apps from one chat in your browser. Tell it what you need in plain English. It drafts a survey and leaves it inactive for you to review, maps its questions to CRM fields, adds and updates accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities, answers questions about your pipeline, takes backups, and finds out why responses stopped reaching the CRM. It waits for your yes before it sends, deletes or changes many records. On a paid plan, the price of the AI Assistant includes an AI usage allowance, and you can buy more. On a free trial, or with free services, you use it with your own Claude subscription. You can turn it on or off. You own your data; Clouve hosts the containers.
