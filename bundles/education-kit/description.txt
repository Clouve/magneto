Education Kit (with AI Assistant) — clv-docker-compose.yml:
Moodle and Gibbon provide comprehensive tools that enhance both learning and administrative efficiency in the education sector. Moodle, a leading learning management system, offers a flexible platform where educators can create tailored courses featuring interactive content, assessments, and collaborative tools, facilitating an engaging and effective online learning environment. Gibbon, on the other hand, focuses on streamlining school operations, supporting the management of student records, timetables, and staff communication, which allows educators to dedicate more time to student engagement and teaching. Together, these platforms empower educational institutions to deliver a seamless educational experience and manage their resources more efficiently. Paired with Clouve's AI Assistant, the Education Kit goes a step further—bringing intelligent automation, conversational guidance, and AI-driven productivity directly into the workflows of administrators, teachers, and learners, so institutions can accelerate course creation, automate routine tasks, and unlock smarter insights across both platforms.
