Extended: A student records system and a learning platform for schools, already connected to each other. Gibbon runs the school office: student records, timetables and attendance. Moodle™ runs teaching: courses, assessments and grading. Moodle is set up to use Gibbon's student and staff accounts, courses and enrolments. You tell the AI Assistant what you need in plain English, in one chat in your browser that works on both apps. It upgrades either app when you choose, installs Gibbon modules and Moodle plugins, takes and restores backups, moves Gibbon to the next academic year, and finds out why a page shows an error. By default it asks before it changes anything. On a paid plan, the price of the AI Assistant includes an AI usage allowance, and you can buy more. On a free trial, or with free services, you use it with your own Claude subscription. You can turn it on or off. Your data stays yours. Moodle™ is a registered trademark of Moodle Pty Ltd.
